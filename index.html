02 — Featured research

Fighter aging curves in the UFC

2026 Donald Nasca Award — $2,500 fully funded

When do athletes peak, and when do they decline?

Building empirical aging curves for UFC fighters — analyzing striking accuracy, takedown success rate, and win rate across full careers to identify peak and decline points, and whether that trajectory shifts by fighting style or weight class. Faculty-sponsored by Dr. Megan Johnson.

Data collected and cleaned from UFCStats.com and Kaggle using Python (pandas, BeautifulSoup); aging curves modeled with the delta method and mixed-effects regression in R. Selected for presentation at the 2027 OSCAR Student Research and Creativity Exposition, with plans to submit to NCUR 2027 and a peer-reviewed sports analytics journal.

Python R Mixed-effects regression Delta method pandas / BeautifulSoup
